146,461 games played in Core Bo1 during Nov 8 - 14, 2026 among all ranks, across 9,191 players.

## Most played

The five most-played archetypes, by share of all decks played:

- Amber/Sapphire Detectives — 10.6% of the meta, 53.2% win rate
- Emerald/Steel Midrange — 9.4% of the meta, 52.6% win rate
- Amber/Emerald Circle of Life — 8.2% of the meta, 50.5% win rate
- Amber/Sapphire Control — 5.0% of the meta, 48.9% win rate
- Amber/Amethyst Midrange — 4.6% of the meta, 51.9% win rate

## Winning

The best win rates among archetypes with at least 100 games:

- Amber/Emerald Princess Aggro — 54.4% win rate on 3.1% of the meta
- Amber/Steel Rockstar Aggro — 54.3% win rate on 2.0% of the meta
- Amber/Amethyst Aggro — 53.4% win rate on 1.8% of the meta
- Emerald/Steel Touch Paradise — 53.2% win rate on 2.2% of the meta
- Amber/Sapphire Detectives — 53.2% win rate on 10.6% of the meta

And the lowest among archetypes with at least 1% of the meta:

- Amber/Ruby Candle Control — 48.2% win rate on 2.4% of the meta
- Amber/Sapphire Control — 48.9% win rate on 5.0% of the meta

## Moving

From Nov 1 - 7, 2026 to Nov 8 - 14, 2026, the meta is moving —

Gaining ground:

- Amber/Amethyst Midrange — 4.6% (up from 2.7%)
- Amethyst/Steel Midrange — 3.8% (up from 2.4%)
- Amethyst/Emerald Retro Device — 4.4% (up from 3.2%)

Losing ground:

- Amber/Emerald Circle of Life — 8.2% (down from 10.8%)
- Emerald/Steel Touch Paradise — 2.2% (down from 3.9%)
- Amber/Sapphire Control — 5.0% (down from 6.4%)
