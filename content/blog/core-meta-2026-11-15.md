127,264 games played in Core Bo1 during Nov 15 - 21, 2026 among all ranks, across 8,506 players.

The five most-played archetypes, by share of all decks played:

- Amber/Sapphire Detectives — 13.3% of the meta, 52.4% win rate
- Emerald/Steel Midrange — 7.2% of the meta, 52.8% win rate
- Amber/Emerald Circle of Life — 6.5% of the meta, 50.3% win rate
- Amber/Steel Rockstar Aggro — 5.9% of the meta, 55.1% win rate
- Amethyst/Emerald Retro Device — 4.7% of the meta, 49.1% win rate

## Moving

From Nov 8 - 14, 2026 to Nov 15 - 21, 2026, the meta is moving —

Gaining ground:

- Amber/Steel Rockstar Aggro — 5.9% (up from 2.0%)
- Amber/Sapphire Detectives — 13.3% (up from 10.6%)
- Emerald/Ruby Action Retro — 2.3% (up from 0.5%)

Losing ground:

- Emerald/Steel Midrange — 7.2% (down from 9.4%)
- Amber/Amethyst Remember Merida — 1.5% (down from 3.3%)
- Amber/Sapphire Control — 3.2% (down from 5.0%)
