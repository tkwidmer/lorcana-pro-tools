162,720 games played in Core Bo1 during Nov 1 - 7, 2026 among all ranks, across 9,775 players.

## Most played

The five most-played archetypes, by share of all decks played:

- Amber/Emerald Circle of Life — 10.8% of the meta, 50.2% win rate
- Amber/Sapphire Detectives — 10.2% of the meta, 52.8% win rate
- Emerald/Steel Midrange — 9.2% of the meta, 53.3% win rate
- Amber/Sapphire Control — 6.4% of the meta, 48.5% win rate
- Amethyst/Ruby Midrange — 4.7% of the meta, 51.9% win rate

## Winning

The best win rates among archetypes with at least 100 games:

- Amber/Amethyst Aggro — 54.8% win rate on 2.1% of the meta
- Amber/Steel Rockstar Aggro — 54.7% win rate on 2.3% of the meta
- Emerald/Steel Touch Paradise — 54.1% win rate on 3.9% of the meta
- Emerald/Steel Midrange — 53.3% win rate on 9.2% of the meta
- Amber/Emerald Princess Aggro — 52.9% win rate on 2.7% of the meta

And the lowest among archetypes with at least 1% of the meta:

- Emerald/Ruby Action Retro — 46.8% win rate on 1.8% of the meta
- Emerald/Ruby Ruby R.E.D. — 47.4% win rate on 2.5% of the meta
- Amber/Sapphire Control — 48.5% win rate on 6.4% of the meta

## Moving

From Oct 25 - 31, 2026 to Nov 1 - 7, 2026, the meta is moving —

Gaining ground:

- Amber/Sapphire Detectives — 10.2% (up from 7.9%)
- Amethyst/Ruby Midrange — 4.7% (up from 2.7%)
- Amber/Emerald Circle of Life — 10.8% (up from 9.1%)

Losing ground:

- Emerald/Steel Touch Paradise — 3.9% (down from 5.4%)
- Amethyst/Steel Midrange — 2.4% (down from 3.9%)
