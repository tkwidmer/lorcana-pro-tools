# Tournament tools and the Tournament History archive

Read before touching Tournament Lookup, Store Lookup, Field Breakdown, the `/results/*` pages, `api/tournament-history.ts`, `api/_lib/tournament*.ts` or `api/_lib/sideEvents.ts`. The `ravensburger-tournament-api` skill documents the upstream API; `tournament-history-sync` is the recurring import procedure.

## Tournament Lookup

`TournamentLookupPage` accepts a Ravensburger tournament URL and extracts the event ID. It uses `tournamentApi.js` for all data:
- `fetchEventDetails` → raw event + phases + rounds
- `getTournamentStructure` → resolved current round, top-cut size, rounds remaining, advancement requirements
- `fetchAllStandings` → a round's full standings in one upstream call (`…/standings/`, not the paginated variant)
- `fetchAllRegistrations` → all registered players (paginated loop)
- `analyzeId` → top-cut outlook: exact lock/elimination bounds, the projected cut from `projectCut`, and the ID call in the final round
- `analyzeAdvancement` → status (secured/possible/eliminated) toward the next phase cutoff

Until a round has generated standings (`currentRoundId` is null) the page shows the registered roster instead of standings. That covers all of round 1: RPH posts a round's pairings and results before it generates any standings, so once a round has started (`activeRoundId`) the page also loads its matches and shows the Matches tab beside the Roster, landing on Matches. Gating matches on standings once hid every round-1 pairing of a 778-player DLC.

## Ink pairs (Metagame and Matchups tabs)

Events that show deck descriptors (a phase's `effective_show_deck_descriptor_to_opponents`; DLC London 1254364 does, DLC Bangkok 1233041 does not) put each player's submitted deck's inks on every match as `player_match_relationships[].user_event_status.deck_descriptor_label` (`parts[0].value` "amethyst,ruby"). Standings and registrations do not carry it, and the profile picture (`EmeraldSteel.webp`) is the player's avatar, not their deck. Public on both hosts, no session; in London 1,477 players had one, each the same across all their rounds, and 53 slots had none (no deck submitted). `src/lib/tournamentInks.js` (unit-tested) reads it: `playerInks()` (shown beside names in Standings, Roster and Matches), `inkPairMeta()` (Metagame: players, share and the record against other pairs, mirrors excluded) and `inkPairMatrix()` (Matchups: row pair's match win rate against each column pair, draws as half, mirrors on the diagonal). Byes, assigned losses and unreported matches are skipped. Both tabs appear only when a loaded match has a pair. Each tab ends with **Download Social Media Share**: `src/lib/tournamentLookupShareImage.js` draws the tab as a PNG on a canvas (2× scale) in the site's brand (ink black, forge gold, Oswald and Source Sans 3, the InkbornForge mark, `inkbornforge.com/tournament-lookup`), headed by the event name, format, starting players and the round the data runs through (`eventLine()`); the matchup image adds a vs-field column and fades cells under 5 matches. Its pure helpers are unit-tested; the drawing is checked by rendering it in a browser. `api/tournament.ts` drops the descriptor's `symbols` (signed icon URLs) like the profile images.

## Top-cut outlook

`analyzeId()` in `tournamentApi.js` (ported in `api/_lib/discordTournamentApi.ts`) works from the standings after the last complete Swiss round; `swissRoundsRemaining` counts the round in progress. Exact first: `locked` when fewer than `topCutSize` other active players can still reach the player's points, `eliminated` when that many are already beyond their best finish. With one round left it answers the ID question by counting who could end above the player's points + 1: `safe` if all of them winning still leaves room, `borderline` if about half of the level players must lose, `danger` otherwise. Earlier rounds are `contending`. Every result carries `projectCut()`: the current point distribution halved once per remaining round (Swiss pairs within point groups; draws and the bye are ignored), giving `lockPoints` (the lowest total that fits inside the cut), `bubblePoints` and about how many of the players there make it, plus the wins needed to reach each. Comparing against the current 8th place with rounds still to play was the old behaviour and marked every player of a round-2 event "do not ID".

A standings row mixes two snapshots: `record` and `user_event_status.total_match_points` are live (they move as matches in the round in progress are reported) while `rank`, `match_points`, `match_record` and the tiebreakers are frozen to the round. The page, the analysis and the Discord embeds use the frozen fields so rank, points, record and rounds remaining describe the same moment; `fetchAllStandings` once overwrote `match_points` with the live total, which showed a rank-1 player on 3 points under a rank-2 player on 6.

## Store Lookup

`StoreLookupPage` extracts every UUID from pasted store IDs/URLs and fetches each store via `/api/tournament?type=store`, plus its events (`storeEvents`) and unique-fan count. `storeTiers.js` models RPH's store-tier program:
- `computeTierProgress()` tracks provisional Legendary progress in the pro-rating window (`PRORATE_WINDOW`, `LEGENDARY_PRORATED_REQUIREMENTS`, plus running a Hyperia City Prerelease).
- `computeStandingTierStatus()` gives the standing tier over the trailing 4 set seasons, whose boundaries are derived from Prerelease events (`deriveSeasons()`).

Each lookup writes the extracted store IDs to the URL as `?stores=<id>,<id>` (whether IDs or store URLs were pasted), so the link is shareable; opening it pre-fills the input from that param (over the localStorage last input) and auto-runs the lookup.

The page also shows a static Welcome/Standard/Legendary reference (definitions + benefits from RPH's announcement, requirements from the `*_MAINTENANCE_REQUIREMENTS` constants).

Only `display_status === 'complete'` events count. The window dates and requirements are hardcoded from the Aug 2026 program email, so they'll need updating when RPH changes the program.

## Field Breakdown

`FieldBreakdownPage.jsx` (`/field-breakdown`, supporter-gated): paste every decklist in a room (blank line between lists), or upload carde.io's decklists export (JSON or CSV), and see the room by ink pair (`inkPairField()` in `lib/fieldBreakdown.js`, unit-tested: each deck's two most-played inks, counted) and each list's inks, card count and unmatched names. "Copy for Stream Overlay" copies the ink pairs as `Emerald/Steel 8` lines (`inkPairLines()`), the Stream Overlay Metagame card's input. It needs only the card database; an archetype assignment against duels.ink's profiles was tried first and removed, since the Metagame chart is by ink pair.

The export (the carde.io admin's "Download decklists", JSON or CSV; carde.io dropped the PDF in Nov 2026) is read in the browser by `lib/cardeExport.js` (unit-tested) into this input. The export names a card without its version ("Tod") and the JSON has no set code, so `decklistsFromExport()` resolves each card to a printing whose ink fits the deck's ink pair: the pair covering the most copies, ties broken by the share of each name's printings that fit; the CSV's `card__set_code` narrows a name to that set's printings first. On Quest Coast IV (event 1117326, 27 decks) both files gave the same 27 pairs with every card matched; without the tie-break the JSON's three Zootopia decks tied across every Amber pair. `scripts/carde-field-export.js` (the Stream Overlay panel's "Export carde.io decks" bookmarklet, or pasted into the DevTools console on an event's carde.io admin page) pulls every submitted decklist with the organizer's own session (Play Hub's public API carries no decks), copies the room's ink-pair lines, and leaves the full lists in this page's input format in `cardeField.fieldText`. The textarea persists in localStorage (`lorcana_field_breakdown`).

## Tournament History Archive

The caster-facing tool for surfacing a player's history across multiple imported RPH major events, and any prior head-to-head between two players in a pairing. This is the first feature to store real domain data in Supabase rather than only auth/tier metadata.

**Import (admin-only, one-shot, re-runnable):** `AdminTournamentImportPage.jsx` (`/admin/tournament-import`, linked from `AdminPage.jsx`) posts an event URL to `POST /api/tournament-history?endpoint=import`. Its **Suggested events** card is the bulk path: `?endpoint=suggested-imports` runs `api/_lib/tournamentDiscovery.ts` — the one implementation of major-event discovery (official DLC / Challenge Championship / Last Chance Qualifier templates, the CCQ, ACQ and prize-pool name searches the `tournament-history-sync` skill documents (a name hit needs 32+ players: the substring match also returns CCQ practice nights, feeders and locals), and side events at a DLC or Championship parent's store within three days of it with 200+ players and 5+ rounds) — and returns every discoverable event that is complete, has a round with generated standings (the events list carries each round's `standings_status`, so a store that never entered results is dropped without a per-event fetch), isn't a Ravensburger test fixture and isn't in the archive; the card shows the count and total players, labels each row by category, and **Import all** imports them one at a time. There is no sync script or cache file any more: the archive is the already-imported set. That route (`requireAdmin()` in `api/_lib/requireAdmin.ts`) fetches the event's details, final-round standings, and all completed rounds' matches directly from `RAVEN_BASE`/`HYDRA_BASE` (`api/_lib/tournamentImport.ts` — a server-side port of `tournamentApi.js`'s fetch/pagination logic, since this runs in a Vercel function rather than the browser and can't go through the client-only `/api/tournament` proxy), then upserts them via `api/_lib/tournamentHistorySupabase.ts`. Re-running the import for the same event is idempotent — it always re-derives and upserts, so importing again after more rounds complete updates standings/ranks rather than duplicating rows.

**Schema** (`supabase/migrations/008_tournament_history.sql`): `tournament_history_events`, `tournament_history_standings`, `tournament_history_matches` — zero RLS policies, service-role-only access, same pattern as `discord_favorite_players`/`duels_api_tokens`/`metafy_links`. Player identity is joined by RPH's own stable `player.id` (`rph_player_id`), the same id `TournamentLookupPage.jsx`'s cross-event favorites localStorage and the Team Tracker's memberships already key by — never by name, which is stored only for display/search (trigram-indexed for fuzzy search). "Made top cut" is precomputed at import time (`has_elimination AND rank <= top_cut_size`) rather than needing a separate accomplishments table. Head-to-head lookups use a generated `player_pair` column on `tournament_history_matches` for an O(1) equality lookup instead of an OR'd scan.

**Caster UI:** clicking a non-bye pairing row in `TournamentLookupPage`'s Matches tab (`MatchesTab`'s `onSelectPairing`) opens `PairingHistoryPanel.jsx` — a modal showing a head-to-head banner (prior meetings across imported majors, or "first meeting") plus each player's cross-event summary (events played, top cuts made) alongside their `PlayerMatchHistory` for the currently loaded event. The panel's cross-event data and the loaded event's `PlayerMatchHistory` are two independent data sources shown side by side — this feature does not merge with `/players/:name`'s separate scouted-game/gamelog opponent-profile system (see `docs/agents/duels-data.md`); they remain unrelated.

**Public results pages:** `/results`, `/results/events/:eventId` and `/results/players/:playerId` (catalog tool "Tournament Results", Tournament Tools) expose the same archive without a session — the first free, linkable surface on it. Reads are edge-cached (`Cache-Control: s-maxage`) instead of session-gated, since the data only changes on admin import. `src/lib/resultsArchive.js` (unit-tested) derives a player's outcome per match, the summary stats (record excludes byes; best finish ties go to the larger event; up to 10 most-faced opponents) and adapts archived bracket rows to the RPH match shape `EliminationBracket` renders. Elimination matches are picked by the phase whose `round_type` is `RANKED_SINGLE_ELIMINATION` in `raw_event_details` (Swiss phase names vary per organizer). `event_date` is derived from RPH's `start_datetime` in the event's `timezone` (`eventDateOf()` in `tournamentImport.ts`); migration `019` backfilled it (numbered after `018_day_two_and_player_card.sql`, which landed in parallel) — every earlier import had left it NULL because the code read a field RPH doesn't send. The results list hides events under `MAJOR_MIN_PLAYERS` (32) by default because the discovery sweep also imports tiny CCQ trial events. `PairingHistoryPanel`'s player cards link to the player's results page ("Full history →"). Per-event/per-player sitemap entries and prerendering are deliberately not done (prerender runs against `vite preview`, which has no `/api` functions).

**Day 2 in the archive:** `tournament_history_standings.made_day_two` (migration `018`), precomputed at import like `made_top_cut`: a player made Day 2 if they played a match in the event's second Swiss phase (a DLC's "Day Two Swiss"; matches carry `phase_name`), counted only when that phase holds fewer players than started, since a few side events run everyone through two Swiss phases. `player-history` returns `madeDayTwo` per event and `dayTwoCount`.

**Ink pair per player:** `tournament_history_standings.inks` (migration `028`), the ink pair a player brought to the event: lowercase, sorted, as `resolveColors()` returns them, checked in the database to be one or two valid inks. NULL means unknown. The RPH import fills it from the matches' `deck_descriptor_label` (`playerInksFromMatches()` in `tournamentImport.ts`) for every player who has one, on events that show it; it writes only those players, so a hand-uploaded pair survives a re-import of an event without descriptors. For older events the source is an inkdecks decklist export the owner supplies (a JSON array of decks with `author` and `inks`). An admin uploads it on the event's results page (the "Ink pairs (admin)" card, `InkPairUpload` in `ResultsEventPage.jsx`) to `POST ?endpoint=ink-pairs`, which matches each author to a standing by exact, case-sensitive name (`matchDeckInks()` in `api/_lib/deckInks.ts`, unit-tested), writes the matched pairs (overwriting any earlier pair) and returns the authors it skipped: not in the standings, a name on several players or decks that disagree on inks, or no valid pair. A skipped player is set by hand in SQL. DLC Bangkok 2026 (event 1233041) and the Randolph 5K (1024787) were filled by SQL before the upload existed; in Bangkok the four repeated names were placed by deck-id order, which the upload does not attempt. The standings upsert does not send the column; the import writes it separately through `setStandingInks()`. The `event` endpoint returns `inks` per standing and `ResultsEventPage` shows an Inks column only when some player in the event has one; the `player` endpoint returns `inks` per event, and `ResultsPlayerPage` shows an Inks column and a Decks played card (`playerSummary().inkPairs`: events, top cuts and match record per pair) only when one of the player's events has one. It is the slot a tournament ink-pair matchup grid will compute from, joined with `tournament_history_matches`.

**Majors vs side events:** `event_tier` is the official template (`challenge`, `challenge_championship`, `lcq`, or a generic tier), and CCQs and the large side events of a DLC weekend both sit on generic templates, so `api/_lib/sideEvents.ts` (unit-tested) decides: an event at the same `store_name` as a `challenge`/`challenge_championship` event within three days of it, not on a major template itself, is a side event. A generic-tier event that isn't a side event is a major only at `MIN_MAJOR_PLAYERS` (100) starting players (`isMajor()`): the archive also holds CCQ trials, CCQ side events and third-party locals imported for their head-to-head history (the cost: the sub-100 Asian ACQs and the Asia Championship main event aren't counted). `player-history` flags each event `sideEvent` and `major` and returns `majorsPlayed` beside `eventsPlayed`; `topCutCount`/`dayTwoCount` count majors only. The Stream Overlay player card's "Majors" tile and `PairingHistoryPanel`'s "Majors played" read `majorsPlayed`; side-event finishes still show with a "Side event" tag.

**API** (`api/tournament-history.ts`, `?endpoint=` dispatch; `docs/agents/api-routes.md` lists each endpoint's auth): `import`, `recent-imports`, `suggested-imports` (admin-only), `recompute-ratings` (admin or `CRON_SECRET`), `pairing-badges` (any session), and the public, edge-cached reads `events`, `event`, `event-player`, `player`, `player-history`, `head-to-head`, `search-players`, `ratings`.

## Player ratings

A Glicko-1 rating per RPH player over every archived match (spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-player-ratings-design.md`). Glicko-1, not Glicko-2: volatility adds nothing for players seen in a few bursts a year, and the rating deviation (RD) is what handles bursty data.

- **The model** (`api/_lib/glicko.ts`, pure, tested in `api/_lib/__tests__/glicko.test.ts` against Glickman's worked example): 1500 ± 350 for a new player. One event is one rating period: events run in `event_date` order, ties by `rph_event_id` (numeric), and every match in an event updates against the opponents' ratings as they stood at the start of the event, so round order inside it does not matter; Swiss and top cut both count. Win 1, loss 0, draw 0.5. Byes and the unreported matches (no winner, not a draw, about 3,200 of them) are **skipped, not counted as losses**. Before each of a player's periods their RD grows with days away, `sqrt(RD² + c²·days)` capped at 350, with `c ≈ 18.05` so RD 60 returns to 350 after a year. A winner who is neither player throws.
- **Thresholds:** established means RD ≤ 100 (`ESTABLISHED_RD`), about two events; only established players are ranked. A provisional player shows a rating, never a rank.
- **Storage:** `tournament_history_ratings` (migration `025`, service-role only): one row per player with rating, RD, matches, W/L/D, last event date, the latest name, and `history`, one `{ rphEventId, eventDate, rating, rd }` point per rated event. An index on `(rd, rating desc)` serves the leaderboard and the rank count.
- **Recompute:** always from scratch, since an import can add an older event that changes everything after it. `recomputeRatings()` in `tournamentHistorySupabase.ts` pages every non-bye match and every event in 1,000-row `.range()` pages (supabase-js returns at most 1,000 rows per request), runs `rateEvents()`, upserts in 1,000-row chunks, then deletes rows the run did not write. It runs nightly at 03:00 UTC (`.github/workflows/ratings-recompute.yml`, `CRON_SECRET`) and from **Recompute ratings** on `/admin/tournament-import`, which shows the player count and elapsed time; after an import, ratings are stale until one of those runs. `vercel.json` gives the route 60 s (the Hobby default is 10 s); the read is the cost, the math is milliseconds.
- **Reads:** `ratings` returns the top 100 established players with `delta`, the change since their previous rated event. `player` and `player-history` carry `rating: { rating, rd, matches, rank, established, history }`, `null` for a player with no rated match; `rank` is counted at read time (established players rated higher, plus one) and is null while provisional.
- **Surfaces:** `/results/ratings` (`ResultsRatingsPage`, linked from the Tournament Results header and the catalog), the Rating tile and rating-over-events line on `/results/players/:id`, the Rating tile on the Stream Overlay player card and a `casterPoints()` line ("Rated 1,712, ranked 14th in the archive") for an established player, and the rating in `PairingHistoryPanel`. `formatRating()` and `ratingDelta()` in `resultsArchive.js` format it everywhere on the site.


## Teams

The Team Tracker (spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-team-tracker-design.md`, mockups beside it): a curated directory of teams and dated player memberships over the archive, plus live Play Hub events. `/teams` (`TeamsPage`, catalog tool "Team Tracker") lists every team; `/teams/:slug` (`TeamPage`) shows the roster, the team's results at every archived event a member played while on the team, and the live and upcoming majors with how many of the current roster are registered in each (one registrations crawl per event, client-side); `/teams/:slug/manage` (`TeamManagePage`) is the one roster editor; `/admin/teams` (`AdminTeamsPage`, linked from `/admin`) creates teams and assigns managers. `/results/players/:id` shows a player's current teams as badges and former ones muted, and `/results/events/:id` has a Teams tab (beside Standings and Bracket) listing every team with a member who was on it on the event date, by best finish with the combined record (`teamsAtArchivedEvent()` in `api/_lib/teams.ts`, computed in the `event` read, which is tagged `teams` as well as its event tag).

**Schema** (`supabase/migrations/031_teams.sql`, service-role only like the archive): `teams` (`slug`, `name`, `url`, `logo_url`, `manager_user_id`, `disbanded_on`) and `team_members` (`team_id`, `rph_player_id`, `player_name`, `joined_on`, `left_on`). A player may hold many memberships over time; a partial unique index allows one open (`left_on IS NULL`) membership per player per team. **Membership on a date** is the rule everything hangs on: a member counts for an event when `joined_on <= event_date <= left_on` (`isMemberOn()` in `api/_lib/teams.ts`, unit-tested with the grouping in `api/_lib/__tests__/teams.test.ts`), so a former member stays on past events and drops off later ones. A NULL `joined_on` (migration `032`) is an unknown start and counts from the beginning of the archive: rosters are usually entered long after a team formed, and a "joined today" default hid every past event. The manage page leaves Joined blank by default and shows it as "Since before the archive". Live events always use the current roster.

**Roles.** An admin creates teams, changes slugs and assigns each team a **manager** (any signed-in user, picked by email on `/admin/teams`). The manager and admins edit the roster, name, link and logo; `requireUserWithTier()` + `canEditTeam()` decide, and a manager who sends `slug` or `managerUserId` gets 403 rather than a silent drop. **Logos** are uploaded with the Stream Overlay's `uploadLogo()` into the public `stream-logos` bucket (migration `016`) and saved as `logo_url`; `team-save` rejects any URL outside that bucket's public path. **Dissolving** (`team-dissolve`, manager or admin) calls `dissolve_team()`, which stamps `disbanded_on` and closes every open membership on that date in one transaction; the team is then read-only (every write 409s), leaves Tournament Lookup's picker and sits under "Former teams" in the directory. **Reinstating** (`team-reinstate`, admin only) clears the stamp and reopens only the memberships whose `left_on` equals it, so an earlier leaver stays former. Teams are never deleted; a row made by mistake goes in SQL.

**API** (`api/tournament-history.ts`): public, edge-cached reads `teams` and `team&slug=` (tagged `teams`; every write purges the tag with `dangerouslyDeleteByTag`, so an edit shows on the next request), `live-majors` (the admin import's discovery sweep run through `filterLiveCandidates()`: in-progress events and those starting within 14 days, cached 5 min; `TournamentLookupPage`'s landing and `TeamPage` show it through `LiveMajorsList` + `useLiveMajors`); `my-teams` (session: `{ admin, teams }`, how the team page decides to offer "Manage roster"); `admin-teams` and `admin-profile-search` (admin); POSTs `team-save`, `team-member-save`, `team-member-delete`, `team-dissolve`, `team-reinstate`. `player` gained `teams`. Queries live in `api/_lib/teamsSupabase.ts`; the directory's "last major" runs one standings query over every member of every team (fine for a few hundred members; page it per team if the directory grows past a few thousand). The public team reads never return `manager_user_id` or an email.

**Tournament Lookup.** The old localStorage "My Team" tag (`lorcana_tournament_team`) is gone. The Teams tab ranks every active directory team with a current member in the loaded event by combined match points (`teamsAtEvent()` in `src/lib/teams.js`, over the `teams` read, which carries each team's current `members` for this), with members in the event, combined record, points, best rank and members in the cut, like zero.gg's event teams view; tapping a team drills into its members with the summary tiles (`teamLiveSummary()`). The drilled team's slug rides in the URL as `?team=<slug>` (so a team page deep-links to `/tournament-lookup?event=<id>&tab=team&team=<slug>`), and its roster becomes the `{ [playerId]: { name } }` map the tables, Matches filter, ticker and `EliminationBracket` already read; `TeamBadge` is display-only. Favorites are unchanged. Under the team list, **Download Social Media Share** draws the top 5 teams by their best individual placement (ties: combined points, then name; `topTeamsByBestPlacement()`), each with that player's rank, name and record, the team's logo (loaded with `crossOrigin` from the public `stream-logos` bucket, initials when there is none or it fails), combined record and points, and members playing out of the roster and in the cut (`renderTeamsShareImage()` in `src/lib/tournamentLookupShareImage.js`, beside the Metagame and Matchups images).
